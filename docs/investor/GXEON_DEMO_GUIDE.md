# GXEON Demo Guide

## Demo objective

Use the Vercel dashboard to show GXEON OS as a safe visual demo of an execution operating system. The demo should communicate architecture, workflow, and activation roadmap without claiming live production activation.

## Demo URL

`https://<vercel-preview-or-demo-url>`

## Suggested demo path

1. **Command Center (`/`)** — introduce the OS and safe preview mode.
2. **Radar X (`/radar-x`)** — explain opportunity signal capture.
3. **Task Engine (`/task-engine`)** — show conversion from opportunity to execution queue.
4. **Agent Hub (`/agent-hub`)** — explain human/AI coordination.
5. **Marketplace (`/marketplace`)** — show offers/jobs packaging concept.
6. **Financial Core (`/financial-core`)** — explain revenue tracking and ledger readiness.
7. **Analytics (`/analytics`)** — show outcome intelligence and reporting layer.
8. **Integrations (`/integrations`)** — explicitly note all providers are disconnected.
9. **Settings (`/settings`)** — show activation planning and workspace controls.

## What to say

- "This is a visual demo, not a live production system."
- "APIs are disabled and external providers are not connected."
- "Supabase is the planned data/auth/storage layer, pending secure activation."
- "The primary workflow is Opportunity → Task → Execution → Revenue → Analytics."
- "The first monetization path is a manual-first Freelance Revenue Engine."

## What not to claim

- Do not claim real revenue.
- Do not claim real active users.
- Do not claim active investor commitments.
- Do not claim active Supabase, payment, blockchain, or marketplace integrations.
- Do not claim autonomous production automation.

## Demo success criteria

- Investor understands the OS-level workflow.
- Investor sees a coherent module map.
- Investor understands current limitations.
- Investor can identify the next activation milestone.
