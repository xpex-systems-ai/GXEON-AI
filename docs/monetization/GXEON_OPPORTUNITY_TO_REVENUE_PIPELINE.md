# GXEON Opportunity to Revenue Pipeline

## Pipeline

`Radar X Preview Candidate → Opportunity Inbox REVIEW → QUALIFIED → Proposal Preview → Task Preview → Evidence Plan → Payment Pending`

## Proposal Preview

Proposal previews are `DRAFT_ONLY`. They include a problem summary, recommended solution, delivery steps, estimated effort, suggested price range, risk notes and evidence summary. They are never sent automatically.

## Task Preview

Task previews are `TASK_PREVIEW_ONLY`. They include execution checklists, required connectors, approval gates, forbidden actions, rollback requirements and evidence requirements. They do not execute work.

## Evidence Plan

Evidence plans list required real proof such as before screenshots, after screenshots, PR links, deployment URLs, error logs, acceptance notes and rollback notes. Fake evidence is explicitly disallowed.

## Payment boundary

The Monetization Board now reflects opportunity pipeline counts while keeping revenue at R$0. Checkout readiness remains `NOT_CONNECTED`; checkout session creation and payment capture remain disabled.
