# Objection Handling

## Is this finished?
No. GXEON OS is in active development. Audit OS is the active product line; SaaS and agent economy are future layers.

## Do you have revenue?
No revenue is claimed unless proof exists.

## Is AI safe for this?
The system is manual-first, preview-first, read-only first, and fail-closed.

## Why now?
AI output is abundant; controlled execution, evidence, and monetization discipline are the scarce layer.
