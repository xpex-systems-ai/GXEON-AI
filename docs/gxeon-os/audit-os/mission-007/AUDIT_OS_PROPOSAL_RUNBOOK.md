# Audit OS Proposal + Offer Runbook

1. Open Audit OS Mission Control.
2. Confirm case, evidence, findings, score and report readiness.
3. Choose an official offer card.
4. Click **Criar prévia de proposta** and review/edit the copy.
5. Paste the temporary operator token in memory only.
6. Click **Salvar proposta** to create a `DRAFT` only.
7. Send and negotiate manually outside GXEON OS.
8. Do not claim revenue until a future Revenue Ledger mission records real proof of payment.

Safety boundaries: no auto-send, no checkout, no invoice, no payment provider calls, no fake client, no fake revenue.
