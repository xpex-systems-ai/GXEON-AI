# Traffic Growth Playbook

Organic growth priorities: clear README SEO, build-in-public posts, short educational videos, technical articles, relevant communities, warm partner introductions, and portfolio backlinks.

Allowed tactics: manual posting, thoughtful comments, helpful replies, docs updates, demos, case studies with permission, and UTMs.

Forbidden tactics: bots, fake visits, fake stars, fake forks, fake testimonials, engagement pods, spam DMs, scraped lists, bought traffic represented as organic, and unverifiable revenue screenshots.

Measure views, engagement, conversations, calls, pilots, proposals, and confirmed revenue separately. Revenue requires proof.
