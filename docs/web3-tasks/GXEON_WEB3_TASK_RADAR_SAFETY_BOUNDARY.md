# Web3 Task Radar Safety Boundary

The radar is `PREVIEW_ONLY` and manual-first.

## Disabled actions

- No wallet connection.
- No seed phrase, private key or wallet secret storage.
- No blockchain transaction creation.
- No message signing.
- No reward claim.
- No external platform task submission.
- No account creation.
- No captcha bypass.
- No multi-account behavior.
- No community spam.
- No customer contact or email sending.
- No payment provider calls.
- No database persistence, workers or schedulers in P0.

## Allowed actions

- Static source registry display.
- Manual import of operator-provided public task metadata.
- Deterministic risk and opportunity scoring.
- In-memory internal previews.
- Evidence checklist and next manual action preparation.
